06 — PROCESS

A process built for
clarity and results

Free Discovery Call
01

Discovery Call

Before anything else, we get on a Free Discovery Call to understand your business, your goals, and what a successful project actually looks like for you. No pitching, no pressure, just an honest conversation to make sure we're the right fit.

02

Proposal & Agreement

Within 24 hours you'll have a detailed proposal, clear scope, exact timeline, and transparent pricing. No hidden costs, no vague promises. Once you're happy, a 50% deposit confirms the project and we get started.

03

Design & Strategy

Every page is designed in Figma before a single line of code is written. You see exactly how your website will look and feel, and nothing moves forward until you've approved it. No guessing, no unwanted surprises.

04

Development & Build

Once the design is approved, we build it, clean, optimized, and tested across every device and browser. You get regular progress updates throughout so you're never left wondering where things stand.

05

Review & Refinement

You get full access to a live staging environment to review everything before it goes live. Share your feedback, request changes, and refine until you're completely satisfied. Nothing gets published without your sign-off.

06

Launch & Handoff

We handle the full deployment, domain setup, SSL, speed checks, and go-live. Once live, you receive complete documentation, a recorded video walkthrough, and a training session so you fully understand and own everything we've built together.
